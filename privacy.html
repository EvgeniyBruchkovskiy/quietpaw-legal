Privacy Policy

Quietpaw: Dog Bark Monitor · Bundle ID com.wamabel.quietpaw · Effective 1 October 2026

In short: Quietpaw does not collect any personal data. Sound is analysed on your iPhone and is never sent to me. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers of my own. In App Store terms: Data Not Collected.

Who I am

Quietpaw: Dog Bark Monitor (“Quietpaw”, “the app”) is developed and published by Yevhenii Bruchkovskyi, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). Questions about this policy: thewayprod1@gmail.com.

The microphone

  • Quietpaw uses the microphone only between the moment you tap “I’m leaving” and the moment you tap “I’m back”. At any other time it does not listen.
  • During that time, sound is analysed on the device by Apple’s built-in sound classifier (the SoundAnalysis framework), which recognises sounds such as barking, howling or whining.
  • The audio stream is not recorded in full and is not sent anywhere — not to me and not to any third party.

What is stored on your device

  • Episodes: the time and duration of each episode — barking, howling, whining, growling or a loud noise.
  • Sound levels: one volume value per minute of the session.
  • Clips (Quietpaw Pro only): a short clip of up to 13 seconds for an episode. You can turn clips off in the settings. Clips are kept for 7, 30 or 90 days, as you choose, and within a storage limit; older clips are removed. If human speech is detected in a clip, that clip is not saved.
  • Your voice phrase: if you record a phrase (up to 8 seconds) for Quietpaw to play when your dog barks, it is stored only on your device.
  • Your dog’s name and settings, saved with Apple’s standard storage (UserDefaults).

I cannot see or access any of this data. It stays on your iPhone, is removed when you delete the app, and may be included in your device backups according to your own Apple settings.

Alerts on a second iPhone (optional, Pro)

If you turn on alerts, Quietpaw saves short event records in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so that another iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID can show notifications. Each event contains only:

  • the event type — you left, barking, your dog settled, you’re back;
  • a number of minutes;
  • your dog’s name;
  • the time.

No audio or clips are ever uploaded. The private iCloud database belongs to your Apple ID and I have no access to it. iCloud is provided by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Alerts are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time.

“What helps” (US only)

In the United States, the app has a “What helps” section with links to physical products in an external store, Settle (petsettle.com). Links open in Safari. Quietpaw passes nothing to the store except standard UTM tags in the link (such as utm_source=quietpaw), which show that the visit came from the app and do not identify you. Anything you do or buy there is governed by that store’s own terms and privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. I never receive your name, payment or billing details. The app only asks StoreKit, on your device, whether you have Quietpaw Pro.

What I don’t collect

  • No accounts, sign-up, name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics or advertising tools in the app, and no ads.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No servers of my own: your data is never sent to me.

Permissions

Microphone — needed to listen during an absence. Notifications — only if you turn on alerts on your other iPhone. You can change both in iPhone Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

In Quietpaw, go to Settings → Delete all data, or simply delete the app. Events in iCloud can be deleted in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Quietpaw.

This website

These pages are static and use no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of request, for security and reliability; I do not use them to identify anyone.

Children

Quietpaw is rated 4+. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children. Purchases on a child’s device can be managed with Apple’s parental controls (Screen Time, Family Sharing, Ask to Buy).

Your rights

Since I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on my side — wherever you live, including under the GDPR and similar laws. You stay in full control of the data on your device and in your iCloud. If you email me, your message is used only to reply and will be deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
